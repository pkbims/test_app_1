# Positioning — app_1

**It's basically DecorAI or Interium, but it never invents architecture.**

For someone who has a room they don't like and a photo of it, this restyles that exact
room instead of returning a different one. The flow is the one the category already
taught people — upload a photo, get design options back — but the room's structure is
extracted and locked before anything is generated, so walls, windows, doors, ceiling
height and proportions survive untouched, and the user can see what is being protected.
The wedge is credibility rather than features: the most repeated complaint about every
shipping app in this category is that it hands back a room you don't live in, and this
is the one that doesn't.

**Anchored to:**

> "adds addition windows and doors, changes room configuration and does not use your existing room" — LiaGator

> "it still gives me an image of whole new furnitures and it restructures my whole house, nothing like i asked for." — balls242

## Problem 1 — People with taste have no method. They stall for months or years between inspiration and the first decision.

12 of 45. Reported by people who have already moved in, already browsed, already
bought books — the block is sequencing, not inspiration.

**Evidence**

> "I'm absolutely overwhelmed by tackling how to make it ours. I don't know if I lack vision or style, or both, but I'm totally paralyzed by all the decisions."
> "I don't know how to pull a room together. I don't know if I should start with furniture or paint."
> — Jamie, https://www.houzz.com/discussions/5336495/how-to-start-decorating-a-new-to-us-home

> "I have refined taste and an appreciation for nice furniture and harmonious interiors. But I have so far been hopeless when it comes to execution."
> "I've been in my house for 8 years, and it still hasn't come together."
> "These books tell you what the options are and give you ideas, but don't really tell you where to start or how to proceed."
> — cathleen_ni_houlihan, https://www.houzz.com/discussions/2447859/learning-to-decorate-how-do-you-decorate-a-room

> "We moved into our new home 1 year ago. I just feel like there is still so much to do. I tend to get overwhelmed instead of enjoying the process."
> — laurience, https://www.houzz.com/discussions/2650456/do-you-ever-feel-overwhelmed-with-decorating

> "So after 6 months of ignoring, overthinking, and ignoring some more, its time for us to fix up the entry"
> — drivenbydesign, https://www.houzz.com/discussions/2919949/need-help-from-the-houzz-dilemma-experts

> "I thought I was good at home decoration, but now I see that I was mistaken. I'm feeling quite overwhelmed and could really use some guidance."
> — Eml Gns, https://www.houzz.com/discussions/6446213/living-room-ideas

> "Any help would be much appreciated I'm COMPLETLEY lost!!!!"
> — Ashley K, https://www.houzz.com/discussions/6147750/

> "I need help decorating my living room. I have no idea where to start."
> — https://www.houzz.com/discussions/4242041/i-need-help-decorating-my-living-room-i-have-no-idea-where-to-start

**Competitors**

- **Havenly** — $99–$299 per room for a human designer plus a shoppable product list. Solves the method problem by outsourcing it, then monetizes the furniture.
  > "Havenly has overpromised and underdelivered throughout my entire first experience with them. I made an order in April 2026; it's now August, 5 months later, and I'm still waiting for an accent chair"
  > — Mellanie Perez, https://www.trustpilot.com/review/havenly.com

- **Houzz** — free forum where strangers answer. Free and human, but slow, and answers are opinions rather than a sequence. Every quote above is someone who went there and was still stuck.

- **Modsy** — same problem, 3D renders plus designers. Shut down July 2022 owing customers refunds.
  > "$50,000 of in-process orders that are in limbo."
  > — customer quoted in https://techcrunch.com/2022/07/17/modsy-quietly-shut-down-while-some-customers-were-still-awaiting-refunds/

## Problem 2 — AI design tools redesign a room you don't have. They hallucinate new windows, walls and furniture instead of working with the actual space.

7 of 45, all from reviews of shipping AI decor apps. The complaint is consistent
across four different apps and is the single most repeated criticism of the category.

**Evidence**

> "The app advertises that you can take a photo of a room and have it rearrange the furniture and items into a new design. Unfortunately, that is not how the app actually works."
> — 1neVerkn0ws, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ai-interior-design-interium/id6499216812

> "The app doesn't really 'rearrange' anything...it still gives me an image of whole new furnitures and it restructures my whole house, nothing like i asked for."
> — balls242, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ai-interior-design-interium/id6499216812

> "adds addition windows and doors, changes room configuration and does not use your existing room"
> — LiaGator, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/decorai-ai-interior-design/id6471409676

> "It adds windows and doors, strange configuration and it doesn't seem to take any text prompt into consideration"
> — Ellr.H, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/decorai-ai-interior-design/id6471409676

> "does what it wants, changes your photos to what it wants even when you give direction"
> — loveulo13, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/decorai-ai-interior-design/id6471409676

> "this is just terrible. Will often leave room bare and only change a rug with a room that had no furniture"
> — Jully Collette's, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/decorai-ai-interior-design/id6471409676

**Competitors**

- **DecorAI** — photo-to-render AI. Charges before you can evaluate output.
  > "They want to charge you before the you're able to determine at all whether what the app offers is worth anything"
  > — Isabella_belli, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/decorai-ai-interior-design/id6471409676

- **Interium** — same category, same complaint, plus billing friction.
  > "There's a free version but it's a hoax because you don't get enough 'credit' to use a single feature of the app without purchasing premium."
  > — AquinasBrat, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ai-interior-design-interium/id6499216812

- **DecorMatters** — manual drag-and-drop with real catalogue items, so no hallucination, but the editing is weak and it has drifted toward a game.
  > "The app cannot tell the difference between the item and the background & automatically deletes major parts of it."
  > "Scaling could also be improved, many of the rugs cannot be enlarged to fit the entire room."
  > — CreativePhoenyx, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/decor-matters-home-design-app/id1168709467

## Problem 3 — You cannot tell whether something works in your actual room until it is delivered, and by then it is non-returnable.

6 of 45. Distinct from Problem 2: these people made a decision, spent real money,
and the feedback arrived too late to act on.

**Evidence**

> "I bought a new sofa and side chair. They were delivered today and I don't like them for the space. I spent a ton of money and can't get a dime back from the store."
> — TLB Goldman, https://www.houzz.com/discussions/1134716/bought-me-furniture-and-i-hate-it-can-t-return-it-help

> "I am trying to return 2 of them because they don't work in the reality of my home, and am being told that there is a 70% restocking fee!"
> — Rypodmom, https://www.trustpilot.com/review/havenly.com

> "I purchased this couch and coffee table which were delivered a few days ago. They are beautiful pieces and the couch is extremely comfortable, but they are too large for my living room space."
> — Peggy Jackson, https://www.houzz.com/discussions/609820/i-misjudged-furniture-i-bought-online-and-can-t-return-it-help

> "We painted it Revere Pewter in 50% saturation and it just seems SO dark. Lesson learned about painting before you fully understand the space."
> — Ashley K, https://www.houzz.com/discussions/6147750/

> "We went for couches that were very comfortable instead of the most fashionable so this is definitely adding to the difficulty of finding pieces that coordinate well."
> — Taylor, https://www.houzz.com/discussions/5577723/in-desperate-need-of-help-decorating-new-home

**Competitors**

- **Havenly** — recommends products, but the return path is the customer's problem. Restocking fees up to 70% reported above.
- **IKEA Kreativ / Room Planner** — scan-your-room AR, restricted to one retailer's own catalogue, so it cannot answer "will this specific sofa I found elsewhere fit."
- **Amazon / Wayfair "View in Room" AR** — single item at a time, no whole-room composition, and widely ignored.
